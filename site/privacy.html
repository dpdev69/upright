Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Upright doesn't collect, sell or send your data anywhere. There's no account, no analytics and no telemetry, in the app or on this website.

Who is responsible

Upright is published by Basalt (hellobasalt.dev), an independent software studio. Because the app collects nothing and sends nothing, Basalt receives no personal data through it. For any privacy question or request, write to support@hellobasalt.dev. For anything else about Basalt, write to hello@hellobasalt.dev.

The Upright app

Upright runs entirely on your Mac. It doesn't phone home or collect anything.

  • What it talks to. Upright only opens Logitech HID interfaces, to read your mouse's buttons, battery, connection, firmware and serial number and to show them to you. Keyboards are never opened, so it never reads what you type.
  • What it watches on your Mac. To apply per-app profiles, Upright notes which app is in front (its name and bundle ID). Some gestures and scrolling settings use your pointer movement and scroll events. All of this is processed on your Mac while the app runs. Only the apps you add a profile for are kept, as part of your settings, and none of it is sent anywhere.
  • What it stores. Your button mappings, per-app profiles, mouse nicknames, pointer speeds and other settings are kept in Upright's preferences on your Mac (io.github.upright.Upright). Nothing is written to the mouse's memory.
  • What leaves your Mac. Nothing, unless you choose to. Export… saves your mappings to a JSON file where you pick, and Copy Device Report copies your mouse's details to the clipboard so you can paste them into a bug report if you want to.
  • Actions you assign. Open URL and Open App go through macOS, and Run Shortcut starts Apple's shortcuts tool. What happens next is up to the app, site or shortcut you picked.

Permissions

  • Accessibility, to send the keyboard shortcuts, clicks and media keys your buttons are mapped to, and to change scrolling.
  • Input Monitoring, only if macOS hides a mouse from Upright, which can happen over Bluetooth.
  • Notifications, for low-battery alerts. macOS asks for this the first time.

You can turn any of these off in System Settings → Privacy & Security. To remove Upright's settings and permissions completely, follow the uninstall steps in the install guide.

This website

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and loads no third-party fonts or embeds. GitHub, which hosts the site and the downloads, may log technical details such as IP addresses to run and secure its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version is published here and the history is in the repository. For questions or requests about your data, email support@hellobasalt.dev. Bugs and feature ideas are welcome as a GitHub issue.
